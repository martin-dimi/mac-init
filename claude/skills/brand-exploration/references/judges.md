# Casting the 5 judges

The judges stand in for real people landing on the page cold. They decide the ranking. They are not designers and are not the user.

## Rules for casting
- Derive them from the research (who actually talks about this kind of product, where, in what words) and from intake question 10.
- Together the 5 must cover the real target audience: the core fan, the adjacent genre fan, the platform/category player, the lifestyle user who isn't a gamer/fan, and the taste-maker who'd share it (or whatever split fits the product).
- Each persona is specific: name, age, where they hang out online, what they've bought or played, phone habits, what makes them download, what makes them bounce, an AI-sentiment line if relevant, and how they'd browse a landing page (skim on a phone on the bus? read everything on desktop?).
- Make them disagree. At least two should care about different things (e.g. lore fidelity vs "will I actually use this on my walk").
- No persona is a designer or brand critic. Craft only matters to them as far as it makes them trust the product.
- Each persona gets a primary device (at least 3 of 5 judge mainly on mobile).

## Example cast (Dungeon Crawler run)
1. **The DCC superfan** - audiobook listener, quotes the System, active on r/DungeonCrawlerCarl. Wants it to feel like the books without being a cash-in, and is allergic to AI slop.
2. **The LitRPG / progression fantasy reader** - reads Royal Road, likes stats and level-ups, hasn't necessarily read DCC. Asks whether the progression is real.
3. **The mobile location-game player** - plays Pokémon GO / Pikmin Bloom / Orna, compares everything to those, cares about battery, retention and whether it's fun daily.
4. **The walker who isn't a gamer** - walks the dog or goes on evening walks with podcasts. Asks whether this makes walks better and whether it's easy. Gets put off by "gamer" signals.
5. **The indie game taste-maker** - follows indie game sites and shares cool things. Asks whether it's original and well made, and whether they'd post it.

## judges.md format (per judge)
```
## J1 - <name>, <one-line identity>
- Who: ...
- Device + how they browse: ...
- Wants: ...
- Bounces on: ...
- Download trigger: ...
- Voice sample (how they'd describe a site to a friend): "..."
```
