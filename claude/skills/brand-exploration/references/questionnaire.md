# Intake questionnaire

Ask all of it in ONE message. Tell the user that rough answers are fine, that skipped questions get sensible defaults, and that after this the run is autonomous to the end. If a previous run exists, ask question 2. Otherwise drop it.

1. **Product in one breath:** what is it, who is it for and what's the one thing a visitor must get? (Point at a spec file if one exists.)
2. **Previous run (if any):** rank the earlier sites and give each one line on what to keep and what to kill. What were you hoping for that you didn't get?
3. **Priorities:** rank the brand system (logo, type, colour, art style), how the app looks and feels on the phone, and the landing page itself.
4. **Source material dial:** if it's inspired by something (book, game, genre), should it be a clear homage fans recognise or our own thing that only nods to it?
5. **Art style:** is anything required or banned (e.g. pixel art yes or no, and which kind)? Photography, illustration, 3D?
6. **Mood:** 3 words for how it should feel, and 2-3 things you hate (looks, clichés, competitor styles).
7. **Landing page shape:** a short and punchy page or a rich interactive one? Any length limit? What's the CTA (waitlist, download, pre-order)?
8. **References:** image files, screenshots or URLs of things you like (any field). Say what you like about each if it isn't obvious.
9. **Naming:** fixed name, a fixed name plus a sub-name per direction, or open?
10. **Judges:** who is the target audience? (Used to cast the 5 judge personas. Name any group that must be represented.)
11. **Scale and budget:** how many directions (default 5 + 1 learned), and any token, time or money cap? Paid APIs allowed (voice, image generation)?
12. **Hard constraints:** platform (iOS only?), legal/IP lines, words or claims to avoid, writing style rules.
